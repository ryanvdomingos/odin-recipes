How to make this food

First you need this:

  • pasta
  • Sauce Base
  • Greens
  • Finishing Touches

To cook you need make this

  1. Combine pasta, water, broth, tomatoes, oil, Italian seasoning, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, and crushed red pepper in a large pot
  2. Cover and bring to a boil over high heat.
  3. Uncover, reduce heat to medium-high, and cook, stirring frequently, for 10 minutes.
  4. Stir in kale or spinach and cook for 5-7 minutes more, until most of the liquid is absorbed and the pasta is tender. (If using spinach, add it after 10 minutes to avoid overcooking.)
  5. Stir in fresh basil.
  6. Serve immediately, garnished with Parmesan cheese.
