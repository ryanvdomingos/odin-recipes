Things to know to make a delicious casserole.

You need follow this steps

Ingredients:

  • Protein
  • Starch
  • Vegetables
  • Sauce
  • Topping

Cooking:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Grease a casserole dish.
  3. Mix all ingredients (except topping) in a large bowl.
  4. Transfer to the dish, top with your chosen topping.
  5. Bake for 30-60 minutes, or until bubbly and golden, checking for doneness of raw ingredients.
